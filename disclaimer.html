Vehicle Data & AI Disclaimer

Last updated: September 28, 2026.

Vehicle data disclaimer

NYC Auto Pro compiles and explains information from public and, when authorized, commercial third-party sources. Data can be incomplete, delayed, incorrect or unavailable. No report guarantees a clean title, accident-free history, correct mileage, absence of liens, lack of recalls or good mechanical condition.

Recall disclaimer

The free recall feature may use NHTSA campaign data based on decoded year/make/model. It may not determine whether a particular VIN has already received the recall repair. Confirm VIN-specific open recall status directly with NHTSA, the manufacturer or an authorized dealer.

Public photo disclaimer

Public photo searches may link to third-party websites. Publicly viewable does not mean freely licensed. Copyright and other rights remain with the respective owners. NYC Auto Pro should not copy, host or resell third-party photos without permission.

AI disclaimer

Automated customer-support and buyer-guidance responses can be wrong or incomplete. They do not replace a qualified mechanic, attorney, title professional, insurer, lender or other appropriate professional.

Market-value disclaimer

Price estimates and market comparisons are informational only. Actual value depends on condition, mileage, location, options, title/history, supply, demand and negotiation.

Trademarks and affiliations

CARFAX®, AutoCheck® and other third-party names are trademarks of their respective owners. Reference to a provider does not imply affiliation, sponsorship, access or endorsement unless NYC Auto Pro has a current authorized agreement.

Independent inspection

Always inspect the vehicle and obtain an independent pre-purchase inspection before purchase. Verify the VIN on the vehicle against the title/registration documents and confirm ownership and lien status.
