Important: This sample demonstrates format and buyer guidance. It is not a verified CARFAX, AutoCheck, NMVTIS, title, accident, auction or ownership report for this VIN. Unavailable information must be obtained from an authorized provider.

Vehicle identification

Example decoded vehicle: 2023 BMW M440i xDrive, coupe, gasoline, 3.0L six-cylinder, AWD. Final reports should verify decoded fields against NHTSA/manufacturer data.

Recall review

NYC Auto Pro can display NHTSA recall campaigns associated with the decoded year/make/model. Customers should confirm VIN-specific open recall status directly with NHTSA or the manufacturer/dealer.

History fields to verify

  • Title status and title brands
  • Lien status
  • Reported accident/damage events
  • Odometer records
  • Service/maintenance records
  • Auction/listing history and authorized photos

Buyer questions

  • Why are you selling the vehicle?
  • Has it had collision or structural repairs?
  • Can you provide maintenance records?
  • Are there any liens or title issues?
  • Can I arrange an independent pre-purchase inspection?

Buyer guidance

Do not rely on a single history report. Compare records, inspect the vehicle physically, verify title/ownership, confirm recall status and use a qualified independent mechanic before purchase.
